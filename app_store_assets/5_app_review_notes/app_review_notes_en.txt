CAMERA PERMISSION JUSTIFICATION & BARCODE SCANNER TESTING:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Why Camera Permission is Required:
   The camera permission is strictly used by halaqoh teachers to scan the physical barcode / QR code printed on student identification cards during attendance check-ins. No facial recognition or biometric photo capture is performed.
   A manual checklist is always available as an alternative so attendance can be completed without scanning.

2. How to Test Barcode Scanning:
   We have attached a sample test card image ("dummy_santri_barcode.png") to this review submission.
   - Open the image on a secondary device screen (or print it).
   - Log in using the Teacher demo account.
   - Navigate to Presensi > tap "Scan Barcode".
   - Point the device camera at the test barcode to observe instant recognition and attendance status confirmation.
