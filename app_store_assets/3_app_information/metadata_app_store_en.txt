9. FULL DESCRIPTION (Max 4,000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MyHalaqoh is an integrated Quranic memorization (tahfidz) tracking and halaqoh management platform designed specifically for Pesantren Tahfidz Al-Qur'an (PPTQ) Luqman Al Hakim Surabaya.

The application connects halaqoh teachers (ustadz), boarding school administration, and parents (wali santri) to create a modern, transparent, and accurate learning ecosystem. Daily mutaba'ah records and prayer attendance are now fully digitized and synchronized in real time from smartphones.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KEY FEATURES FOR HALAQOH TEACHERS (USTADZ)
════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Rapid Attendance & Barcode Scanner:
  Record student halaqoh attendance across daily prayer sessions (Fajr/Shubuh, Dhuha, Asr, Maghrib) in seconds using the built-in student ID card barcode scanner or a rapid manual checklist.

• Precision Memorization Tracking (Ziyadah & Muraja'ah):
  Log daily memorization submissions with Surah selection, Ayah range, Tajweed accuracy, fluency scoring, and custom evaluation notes.

• Structured Curriculum & Target Tracking:
  Monitor student progress based on the semester curriculum evenly divided across national assessment periods (PTS 1, PAS 1, PTS 2, PAS 2) for both Regular and Takhassus programs.

• 1-Juz Tahfidz Certification Management:
  Register eligible students who have completed target Juz for official 1-Juz certification examinations in a single sitting, complete with official grading and criteria.

• Official PDF Reports:
  Export halaqoh attendance summaries and comprehensive student mutaba'ah reports into printable PDF documents.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KEY FEATURES FOR PARENTS & GUARDIANS (WALI SANTRI)
════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Real-Time Monitoring Dashboard:
  Follow your child's memorization growth and daily halaqoh presence at the boarding school from anywhere, at any time.

• 30 Juz Visual Progress Tracking:
  View transparent progress bars per Juz and per Surah, providing complete clarity on completed milestones and upcoming goals.

• Interactive Attendance History:
  Access daily and monthly attendance calendars to review punctuality across all halaqoh sessions.

• Instant Push Notifications:
  Receive immediate alerts when your child submits new memorization, completes a Juz revision, or passes a certification examination.

• Download Progress Reports:
  Save and print semester tahfidz report cards directly from your mobile device.
