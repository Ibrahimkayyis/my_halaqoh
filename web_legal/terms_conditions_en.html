2. User Accounts and Authorized Roles

MyHalaqoh serves authorized institutional users under the following formal roles:

  • Halaqoh Teachers / Ustadz: Authorized to record student attendance (via camera barcode scanning and manual checklist), input daily ziyadah and muraja'ah memorization logs, track curriculum targets, and nominate students for 1-Juz certification examinations.
  • Guardians / Parents (Wali Santri): Granted monitoring access to follow their child's memorization growth, attendance consistency, exam milestones, and download official progress report cards (PDF).
  • Administrators: Manage institutional master data, halaqoh groupings, curriculum targets, and certification examination schedules.

Users are strictly responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and all activities occurring under their accounts. Sharing account credentials with unauthorized third parties is strictly prohibited.

3. Device Permissions & Camera Access

The application requests specific device permissions necessary for its operational features:

  • Camera: Strictly utilized on Teacher accounts to scan the barcode or QR code on physical student ID cards during attendance sessions. Camera access is never used for unsolicited photo capture or video recording.
  • Push Notifications: Utilized to deliver timely alerts regarding student memorization updates, attendance notifications, and certification results to parents and teachers.
  • Storage / Downloads: Utilized to save student semester memorization report cards in PDF format directly to your device storage.
