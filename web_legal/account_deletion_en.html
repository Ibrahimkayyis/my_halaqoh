⚡ Faster In-App Account Deletion

If you have access to the MyHalaqoh mobile app on your smartphone, you can instantly delete your account without waiting for manual form processing:

Open the Profile tab → Tap Delete Account under the Security section → Confirm "Delete Account". Your account credentials and session will be purged immediately.
