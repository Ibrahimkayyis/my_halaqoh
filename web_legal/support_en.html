Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do teachers and guardians obtain their login credentials?
Teacher (Ustadz) and Guardian (Wali Santri) accounts are provisioned directly by the boarding school administration. Employee IDs (NIP) and Student IDs (NIS) are registered within the institutional database.
Can halaqoh attendance be recorded without using the camera barcode scanner?
Yes. The application provides an instant manual checklist within the attendance screen, enabling teachers to record attendance quickly even if a physical card is unavailable.
How can parents track their child's memorization progress?
Parents can log in to their Guardian account and access the Progress tab to inspect visual 30-Juz completion bars, Surah breakdown, and exam certification milestones.
How can I request account or data deletion?
Users may delete their account directly within the app via Profile > Security > Delete Account, or via our online web deletion portal.
