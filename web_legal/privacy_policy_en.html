Privacy Policy

Halaqoh Monitoring & Student Attendance Mobile Platform (MyHalaqoh)

Pesantren Tahfidz Al-Qur'an (PPTQ) Luqman Al Hakim Surabaya • Last Updated: October 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MyHalaqoh. We are fully committed to protecting and respecting the privacy of all our users, including halaqoh teachers (ustadz), guardians / parents (wali santri), and students at Pesantren Tahfidz Al-Qur'an (PPTQ) Luqman Al Hakim Surabaya.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you access and use the MyHalaqoh mobile application, in compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1.1) and international data privacy regulations.

2. Personal Data We Collect

To support halaqoh management and Quranic memorization tracking, the application collects the following data:

  • Identity & Account Information: Employee Identification Number (NIP), Student Identification Number (NIS), full name, assigned institutional role (Teacher, Guardian, Admin), and securely encrypted passwords.
  • Academic & Memorization Data: Daily prayer attendance records, Quranic memorization submissions (Surah name, Ayah range, tajweed grade, fluency score, revision status), 1-Juz certification scores, and halaqoh group names.
  • Contact & Profile Details: Contact phone/WhatsApp numbers (strictly for institutional parent-teacher coordination) and optional user profile avatars.
  • Device Information & Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device push tokens to deliver real-time notifications regarding student attendance and memorization progress directly to guardians.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

Collected information is used exclusively for educational and institutional operations of the boarding school, specifically:

  1. Facilitating instant halaqoh attendance tracking across morning, afternoon, and evening sessions (including camera barcode scanning).
  2. Documenting and tracking Quranic memorization milestones accurately and transparently.
  3. Providing parents and guardians with real-time academic report cards and daily progress updates.
  4. Authenticating users and enforcing role-based data isolation.
  5. Generating official institutional semester report cards in PDF format.

4. Camera Permission & Usage

MyHalaqoh requests access to the device camera strictly for halaqoh teachers to scan the physical barcode or QR code printed on official student identification cards during attendance check-ins.

Strict Privacy Commitment: The camera feed is processed in real time solely to detect barcode strings. The application does not capture, store, transmit, or process biometric photographs, facial recognition data, or private user media.

A manual checklist is always provided within the app as a complete alternative so attendance can be recorded even if camera access is denied.

5. Third-Party Infrastructure & Security

MyHalaqoh relies on industry-standard Google Cloud / Firebase enterprise infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure token-based user authentication and session management.
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted, role-secured database storage for halaqoh records, attendance, and memorization data.
  • Firebase Cloud Storage: Encrypted object storage for user profile photos and downloadable academic reports.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Encrypted push notifications sent to registered guardian devices.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to any commercial advertisers or third-party brokers.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

In compliance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), users have the full right to initiate account and data deletion at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion:

1. In-App Self-Service: Open the Profile tab → scroll to Session / Security → tap Delete Account → confirm via the authentication dialog.
2. Online Web Portal: Users may also submit an online account deletion request anytime via our web portal:

Open Account Deletion Portal

Effects of Account Deletion:

  • Authentication Credentials: User login credentials in Firebase Auth and profile metadata are permanently and irreversibly purged from active directories.
  • Push Tokens: Device FCM tokens are invalidated and erased from database collections.
  • Institutional Academic Records: Historical halaqoh attendance logs and Quranic memorization milestones are retained in the institution's archived records in accordance with legitimate educational regulatory obligations.

7. Children's Privacy Protection

MyHalaqoh serves an educational boarding school institution. Student accounts and memorization records are strictly managed under the direct supervision of verified teachers and lawful guardians. The application contains zero advertisements, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking scripts.
